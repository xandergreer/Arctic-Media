# Arctic Media — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 29, 2026**

Arctic Media is self-hosted media server software. The Arctic Media apps (including the Roku channel, iOS app, and web interface, together the "Apps") are clients that connect exclusively to an Arctic Media server chosen by you — typically one you run yourself, or one whose owner invited you. The developer of Arctic Media does not operate any central service.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The developer does not collect, receive, sell, or share any personal information. The Apps contain no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party data-collection SDKs. No usage data is transmitted to the developer.

## Data stored on your media server

To provide its features, the Arctic Media server you connect to stores:

- **Account information** — your username and credentials, used to sign in.
- **Watch history and playback progress** — what you watched and where you stopped, used to power features such as Continue Watching and cross-device resume.
- **Device sessions** — a record of devices you have paired (such as a Roku or iPhone), so you can review and revoke access.

This data lives only on that server and is controlled by the person who operates it. If you run your own server, that person is you. The developer has no access to it.

## Data stored on your device

The Apps store a small amount of data locally (for example, in the Roku device registry): the address of your server, an authentication token, and playback preferences such as subtitle settings. Signing out removes the authentication data. This information never leaves your device except to communicate with your own server.

## Third-party services

The Arctic Media server may fetch artwork and metadata (posters, episode descriptions) from The Movie Database (TMDB). Those requests are made for catalog enrichment and are subject to TMDB's own privacy policy. The Roku platform itself may collect information as described in Roku's privacy policy, which is independent of Arctic Media.

## Children

Arctic Media is a general-purpose media player and is not directed at children. The content available through it is solely whatever the server's operator has placed in their own library.

## Your choices

Because all data resides on the server you connect to, requests to view or delete your account, watch history, or device sessions should be directed to the operator of that server, who can manage them through the server's administration tools.
